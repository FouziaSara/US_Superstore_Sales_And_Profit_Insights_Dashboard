# US_Superstore_Sales_And_Profit_Insights_Dashboard

1. Superstore Sales And Profit Analysis
An interactive dashboard exploring and giving insights of trends of sales and profits.

2. Short Description :
This project has three dashboards. The first one is called Executive Summary which displays the total number and amount of sales and profits, and trends over time. Then the second dashboard 'Regional Insights' shows the states and regions having the most sales and  monthly trends in different regions. Finally, the third dashboard 'Product Insights' gives us the insights of the category and sub-category contributing the most in product sales and profits, the top 10 products in terms of sales and thw least 10 products cauing losses. This tool is intended for helping the business executives to lower the losses and making improvements in business.

3. Tech stack
The dashboard was built using the following technologies:
Tableau Public- Primary tool for data visualization & dashboard creation
Excel- Used for raw data storage & cleaning
Calculated Fields in Tableau (Profit Ratio, KPIs, Category Filtering)

 4. Data Source
    Source: https://community.tableau.com/s/question/0D54T00000CWeX8SAL/sample-superstore-sales-excelxls

5. Features
   Business Problem: A superstore needs a way to analyze sales, profit, product performance, and regional trends to support strategic decisions.
   Goal of the Dashboard: The goal is to undestand the trends and dynamics of regions and category in terms of sales and profits.
   Walkthrough of Key Visuals:
   Main KPIs: Sales, Profits, Number of Orders, Profit Ratio, and Unique Products
   Visuals: Sales Trend Over Time, Sales By Category, Profit Distribution, Monthly Trend By Region, Category Performance, Sale vs Profit, Discount Impact, Top 10 Products by Sales, and Bottom 10 Products by Profit
   Insights: West region got the highest profit, Technology category contributed most to the sales, and Phones were the most sold product.
